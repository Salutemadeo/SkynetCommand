You say a command ("hellbomb", "orbital laser", "resupply") and the app presses the keys in the game for you.
The speech AI (Whisper) runs on your own PC - nothing is sent to the internet.

INSTALL (one time, needs internet, ~5 minutes, Windows 10 or 11)
  1. Unzip this whole folder somewhere you like (e.g. Documents). Do not run it from inside the zip.
  2. Double-click  "1 - SETUP (run once).bat"
     It downloads a private copy of Python and the speech engine INTO THIS FOLDER.
     Nothing is installed in Windows; your other programs are not touched.
  3. When it says DONE, Skynet Command starts. The very first start downloads the speech model once.

START IT
  Double-click  "Skynet Command"  (the eye icon) - or "Skynet Command.bat".

NVIDIA GRAPHICS CARD? (optional, ~1 GB)
  Double-click "GPU boost (NVIDIA only).bat" - the bigger, more accurate model then runs on your graphics card.
  If your game's FPS drops when you speak: OPTIONS -> PERFORMANCE (AI LOAD: low) or a smaller WHISPER MODEL.

QUICK TIPS
  - Push-to-talk: OPTIONS -> LISTEN MODE: ptt, then pick a key (a mouse side button works great).
  - Click the eye (top left) to switch auto-detect game on/off. Click the first symbol top right to switch the speech AI off/on.
  - Lower OPTIONS -> MACRO SPEED (e.g. 30-35 ms) for faster stratagems; raise it if the game misses arrows.
  - A command not recognised? Click its line in the COMMAND LOG to teach it.

UNINSTALL
  Delete this folder (and the Desktop shortcut if you made one). That's all.

Everything lives in this folder:  app\ (the program, your settings, profiles)  runtime\ (private Python)  models\ (speech AI)
